# Composition studies

Three high-fidelity structure studies were evaluated against the fixed Omarchy console identity.

## A — Control room (selected)

A dense scoreboard and history field occupy the working canvas while a persistent analyst rail holds the nightly brief, evidence, and chat. The composition makes comparison primary without hiding the analyst. On tablet the rail becomes a drawer; mobile becomes a single navigation stack.

## B — Session ledger

The transcript stream leads, with rankings compressed into a command header and analysis appearing inline. This makes forensic work fast but weakens the first-screen leaderboard and long-range comparison.

## C — Analyst briefing

The nightly report leads as an editorial memo with metrics arranged as supporting exhibits. It makes recommendations legible but gives a probabilistic layer too much authority over the underlying evidence.

## Decision

Composition A is selected because the product's unique mechanism is evidence-backed comparison. The analyst remains persistent but visibly advisory, and every conclusion can resolve into the central evidence surface.
